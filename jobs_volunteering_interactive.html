03

Workplace vocabulary

Collocations, key job expressions and differences in usage.

JOB = countable noun

She has a job at a bank. / I’ve applied for three jobs.

Not: She has a work.

WORK = usually uncountable noun

I have a lot of work this week. / I’m doing some voluntary work.

Work is also a verb: I work in IT.

04

Present continuous at work

Build the form first, then select natural verbs and apply the tense.

Formation

am / is / are + verb-ing

Positive: I am working. / She is applying. / They are training.

Negative: He isn’t working today.

Question: Are you working on a new project?

install → installing · gain → gaining · study → studying

When do we use it?

Now: We’re installing software at the moment.

Temporary situations: I’m working on a new campaign this month.

Developing situations: The company is expanding.

Arranged future: We’re meeting the client tomorrow.

Compare: I usually work from home (routine). / I’m working from the office this week (temporary).

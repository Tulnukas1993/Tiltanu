2. Choose the correct form

Present Simple = routine / fact. Past Simple = finished time. Present Perfect = have/has + V3.

1. He ___ sushi every Friday.
2. He ___ sushi yesterday.
3. He has ___ sushi in Japan.
4. She ___ a lot of photos on holiday.
5. She has ___ hundreds of photos.
6. We ___ to Rome last year.
7. I have ___ the Eiffel Tower.
8. She ___ a new backpack last week.
9. They have ___ three emails today.
10. We ___ to Paris yesterday.
11. Have you ever ___ green tea?
12. He ___ to his teacher yesterday.
13. I have ___ to London twice.
14. She ___ at home yesterday.
